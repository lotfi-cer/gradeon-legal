2. What Gradeon does

Gradeon is a tool for managing trading card collections. It lets you:

  • catalogue your cards, their condition and their language;
  • identify them by photographing them;
  • estimate their centering from a photo;
  • consult price estimates drawn from public sources;
  • keep wish lists;
  • compare your collection with another user's and see which trades would be possible between you;
  • update your two inventories inside the app, once a trade has been agreed between you.

Gradeon is an inventory and comparison tool. Gradeon does not buy, sell, ship, authenticate or appraise any card, and takes no part in any transaction.

Gradeon does not put users in contact with each other. The app has no messaging and passes on no contact details: no email address, no phone number, no postal address. Only your username is visible. Getting in touch and arranging a trade, if you want one, happen by means you choose yourself, outside Gradeon.

Prices shown in Gradeon come from public third-party sources (notably TCGplayer and Cardmarket data relayed by pokemontcg.io) and are converted into your currency using exchange rates published by the European Central Bank.

As a result:

  • these prices are indicative estimates, not offers, not quotations, and not appraisal values;
  • they may be out of date, incomplete or wrong, including through an error at the source;
  • they depend on the card having been identified correctly, variant included, which may be wrong;
  • the total value shown for your collection is a simple sum of these estimates and has no inventory or accounting value whatsoever.

Do not base any purchase, sale or trade decision on the figures shown by Gradeon alone. Always check against the marketplaces and, where the stakes justify it, with a professional. We provide no investment advice, and trading cards are not a savings product.

5. Centering and grade estimates are indicative

Gradeon measures a card's centering from a photo and compares it against the grading scales that the grading companies publish themselves on their own websites. The results shown, including where they refer to a PSA, BGS or CGC style scale, are estimates computed by an algorithm from an image.

They are not an official grade, not an appraisal and not a certification. Only the grading companies themselves issue grades, after physically examining the card. Gradeon is not affiliated with, associated with, or endorsed by PSA, Beckett (BGS), CGC or any other grading company.

Their names are used only to identify the public scale a measurement is compared against, which would be meaningless without naming it. Gradeon uses neither their logos, nor their visual identity, nor any data they have not published themselves, and reproduces none of their documents.

The quality of an estimate depends on the quality of the photo. Centering is only one criterion among several in a real grade, which also accounts for corners, edges and surface.
