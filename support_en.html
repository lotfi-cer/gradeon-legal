Gradeon Support

Gradeon is a personal collection-tracking app for trading cards. This page helps you find an answer quickly, or reach us directly.

Contact us

A question, an issue, a suggestion: write to us at contact@gradeonapp.com. We reply directly — there is no form to fill in.

Frequently asked questions

The camera won't open

If you denied camera access by mistake, you can turn it back on from your phone's settings: Settings › Gradeon › Camera. The app also works without a camera, by picking an existing photo from your library instead of taking one on the spot.

How do trades work?

Gradeon does not put users in contact and does not organise any trade. The app lets you compare your collection with another user's, and update your inventory once you have traded your cards yourselves, outside the app.

How do I delete my account?

From the app: Profile › Delete my account. Deletion is immediate, permanent, and does not require writing to us.

Where is my data, and who sees it?

See the Privacy Policy, which details exactly what Gradeon collects and why.
