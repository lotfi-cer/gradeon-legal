Delete my Gradeon account

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Gradeon account (an app published by L. Cerny), what is erased, and what is not. You can do it from the app, or without it, by writing to us.

From the app (quickest)

  • 1. Open Gradeon and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  • 2. Go to the Profile tab.
  • 3. Tap Delete my account.
  • 4. Type your username to confirm, then validate.

Deletion is immediate, permanent and irreversible. You are signed out straight away, and there is no need to write to us.

Without the app, by email

If you have uninstalled Gradeon, changed phones or can no longer sign in:

  • 1. Write to contact@gradeonapp.com from the email address linked to your Gradeon account.
  • 2. Subject: "Account deletion". Include your username if you remember it.
  • 3. We delete the account and send a confirmation to that same address, within one month at most.

Why from that address? It is how we check that the request really comes from you: without this precaution, anyone could have someone else's account deleted. If you write from another address, we will send a confirmation request to the address linked to the account, and we will delete nothing without a reply from it.

If you signed in with Google or Apple, the address linked to the account is the one of that Google or Apple account. If you chose to hide your address with Apple, write to us anyway: we will reply to the relay address provided by Apple, which will reach you.

An account created with "Continue without an account" is linked to no email address and tied to no identity. It can only be deleted from the app; once the app is uninstalled, it is no longer accessible to anyone.

What is deleted

Deletion erases all data linked to your account:

  • your sign-in account (email address, encrypted password, Google or Apple link);
  • your profile: username, country, currency, language and settings;
  • your collection, with the condition, language, finish, grading and purchase price of each card;
  • your collection lists and wish lists;
  • the value history of your collection;
  • your trades, ongoing and completed — they also disappear from your partners' Trades screen;
  • the contribution images and data you had uploaded (scan photos, straightened cards, centering images and measurements).

On the phone, the history of recent scans is erased when the deletion is made from the app. Other settings stored on the device disappear when you uninstall Gradeon.

What is not deleted, and why

  • Cards that moved into another user's collection through a confirmed trade stay there. Their inventory belongs to them, and we do not touch it. Those cards carry neither your username nor any other data about you.
  • Crash reports (Sentry, kept for 90 days) and usage measurement records (PostHog) are tied to no account: they contain neither your email address, nor your username, nor your account identifier. We therefore cannot find them from your account, and they are not affected by its deletion.
  • On Android, the technical data of Google ML Kit (reading the text of cards) is received by Google, not by us, and is not tied to your Gradeon account.

Nothing else is kept: we keep no copy of your account, your collection or your images after deletion. Only possible automatic technical backups from our hosting provider may contain a trace of them, until they are automatically replaced; they are only used to recover from an outage, never to restore a deleted account.

Any question?

Write to contact@gradeonapp.com. The details of the data processed by Gradeon are in the Privacy Policy.
